# Photos still worth taking

The README already has the front, back and side photos, the labelled component picture, the exploded case picture and two videos. These shots would round it out.

## Setup

- Use soft daylight from a window or two desk lamps. Do not use flash.
- Use a plain background such as white paper or a grey mat.
- Prop the phone on books or a tripod and zoom in from further back.
- With the screen on, tap the screen and lower the exposure until the picture is not washed out. Angle the frame so the glass does not mirror a lamp.
- Turn off location tagging on the camera.

## Shots

1. The finished frame on a desk at about 30 degrees, showing a sample photo and the clock. This can replace the three-photo row at the top of the README.
2. The bare board, top and bottom, flat on a plain background with light from above and slightly to the side.
3. The soldered board with the XIAO and breakout fitted, one top view and one angled.

5. All printed parts laid out in assembly order.
6. The frame next to a coin or ruler for scale.
7. The birthday greeting on screen, using a made-up name such as Alex in `birthdays.h`.

## Keep out of the picture

- Personal photos on the display. Use the sample photos.
- Real names, birthdays and Wi-Fi names or passwords, including in the serial monitor and Arduino IDE.
- Your address, calendars and papers in the background.

## Adding them

Resize to about 1600 px wide, put them in `docs/images/` and add them to the README, for example `![Frame on a desk](docs/images/hero.jpg)`.
